About

Our studio

Sunrise Yoga opened in 2018 in the heart of Kitsilano. We keep class sizes small (max 18 students) so instructors can give real attention to every person on the mat. Our 1,800 sq ft studio has radiant floor heating, a changing room with showers, and mat storage for members.

We believe yoga should be accessible, not exclusive. That's why your first class is always free, and our pricing stays honest.
