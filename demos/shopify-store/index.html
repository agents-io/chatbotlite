Our story

Started in a garage in East Van in 2021 with a secondhand sample roaster and an obsession with freshness. We kept things small on purpose. No wholesale, no cafe, no middlemen. Just roasted-to-order beans shipped direct.

Today we source from 6 origin countries, roast weekly, and ship across Canada and the US. Still the same garage. Still the same standards.
